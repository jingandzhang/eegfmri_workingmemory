# Third-party code and licensing

`analysis/fdr_bh.m` attributes its implementation to David M. Groppe and includes a revision history. Its source comments have been preserved. The supplied folder does not include a separate license for this helper; confirm its distribution terms before public redistribution.

`analysis/glob.m` and other shared lab utilities retain their original source text. Confirm ownership and reuse permissions when selecting the repository license. External packages such as SPM, FSL, AFNI, fMRIPrep, REX, and BrainNet Viewer are not bundled and retain their own licenses.

No blanket license has been assigned to the collected code. Authors should select a license for their own contributions and keep third-party notices and applicable exceptions.

The detection folder includes additional contributed code. `detectSO.m` credits Mohsen and Daga (with later inline modifications), and the supplied `edfread.m` is a custom reader with a historical API. Preserve these source notices and establish the applicable redistribution licenses.
